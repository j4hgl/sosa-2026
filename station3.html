STATION 3

Catalytic Stage

The Catalytic Stage highlights the continuous professional growth of BCSTEC educators through trainings, seminars, graduate studies, promotions, and career advancement. These initiatives empower teachers to deliver quality education and foster innovation inside and outside the classroom.

Explore Station 3

🌱 Environmental Stewardship

BCSTEC continues to promote environmental responsibility through sustainable waste management practices. The school's commitment to proper waste segregation, recycling initiatives, and environmental awareness earned recognition as the Champion in the 7th School-Based Solid Waste Management Competition.

Programs and Initiatives

  • Proper Waste Segregation
  • Recycling Activities
  • School Cleanliness Campaigns
  • Environmental Awareness Programs
  • 7th School-Based Solid Waste Management Champion

🚨 Disaster Risk Reduction and Management

The school prioritizes the safety and preparedness of learners and personnel through regular disaster preparedness activities. Earthquake and fire drills, evacuation procedures, and contingency planning ensure that everyone is ready to respond effectively during emergencies.

Preparedness Activities

  • Earthquake Drills
  • Fire Drills
  • Evacuation Plans
  • Contingency Plans
  • Emergency Response Procedures

🤝 Brigada Eskwela

Brigada Eskwela reflects the strong partnership between the school and the community. Parents, alumni, stakeholders, and volunteers worked together to prepare the school before the opening of classes, ensuring a safe, clean, and learner-friendly environment.

Community Partners

  • Parents
  • Alumni
  • Stakeholders
  • Community Volunteers
  • School Personnel

📚 Weekly Learning Matrix (WeeLMat)

To ensure continuous learning during disruptions, BCSTEC implemented the Weekly Learning Matrix (WeeLMat). This initiative provides organized weekly learning tasks that help learners stay on track and continue their studies wherever they are.

Objectives

  • Support continuous learning
  • Provide organized weekly lessons
  • Help learners accomplish learning tasks
  • Ensure learning continuity during emergencies

📻 School Radio Station

The School Radio Station serves as an alternative learning platform and communication channel. It provides educational broadcasts, school announcements, and important information that support learners and teachers throughout the school year.

Purpose

  • Alternative learning delivery
  • Educational broadcasts
  • School announcements
  • Learning continuity
