ABOUT SOSA

State of the School Address 2026

The State of the School Address (SOSA) is an annual event that highlights the school's accomplishments, programs, student achievements, and future goals. This interactive website was developed to help visitors easily navigate the different stations located inside the school gymnasium.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops students into competent, responsible, and innovative individuals through excellence in science, technology, leadership, and character.

Our Vision

Bayawan City Science and Technology Education Center envisions becoming a leading institution that inspires academic excellence, creativity, and service to the community.

Purpose of this Website

This website provides an interactive map of the SOSA venue, allowing guests to easily locate stations and learn more about the exhibits and presentations prepared by the school.
